Make a light show

Add a song and Light Studio splits it into drums, bass, vocals and other instruments, gives each its own part of your strip, and builds a show automatically. Then fine-tune any moment and play it back synced to your lights.

Paint with:
Lanes: each instrument has its own lane and lights only the sections you give it (on any of your lights). Black means "off", so a lane lower in the list (like Accents) shows through only where it's lit. M mutes a lane's lights, S shows only that lane.
Timeline: click to jump · drag the top ruler to set a loop section · double-click a lane to add a cue · drag across lanes to select several cues · drag cues to move them (height = brightness) · Ctrl+scroll to zoom
 Timeline: tap to jump · press and hold in a lane to add a cue · drag a cue to move it · pinch to zoom, two fingers to scroll · drag along the top ruler to set a loop section
 Keys: Space play/pause · C add cue to highlighted lane (tap while playing to mark hits) · ↑↓ change lane · Del delete · Ctrl+C/V copy/paste · Ctrl+Z undo · L loop · [ ] set A/B · ←→ skip 1s (Shift 5s) · Alt+←→ nudge cues · M mute audio · Ctrl+A select all
